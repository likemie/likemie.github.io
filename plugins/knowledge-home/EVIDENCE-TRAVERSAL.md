## Navigation decisions

- Four fixed cases remain. A separate pool draws from 34 institution, network, portal and programme entries.
- Six practice networks, portals and programmes are included in the random library with entry-type labels; the separate services list has been removed.
- International navigation follows explicit cooperation routes: EEN and its partners; Campbell and synthesis methods; OECD, CERI and its research project; BE2 and a founding organization. Labels distinguish organizations, networks, funders and projects.
- The EU initiative and the global teaching-evidence project are context links,
  rather than peer institutions. The 2018 US evidence law and ECNU declaration
  are excluded from the organization draw.
- The historical CCL and Danish cases are explicitly labelled with the 2022
  literature's closure finding. WWC directs readers to the entry's operational
  history without asserting that it is currently reviewing studies.
- EEN's body mentions Queen Rania Foundation, eBASE Africa, Leerpunt, Mizizi Elimu
  Afrika and la Caixa. Index lookup did not find standalone entries for these
  names. The navigation retains the EEN entry as the reading path; it does not
  manufacture institution pages or working links.
- Related research is selected from both outgoing Argument links of topic-specific
  entries and incoming Argument links to them. Broad organizations such as OECD,
  World Bank and GPE do not seed unrelated research.

## Remaining scope

This pass improves discovery of existing entries. It does not establish a complete
world census, verify every institution's present legal or operational status, or
rewrite wiki facts. New standalone institutions need their own source-based wiki
workflow before they can enter the draw.

Additional concept links were checked against the entries' definition or theory-position
passages: teacher research engagement, school enquiry, poor research use,
profession-sensitive research use, four generations of research use, push/pull
knowledge mobilisation and the research knowledge mobilisation model. These are
reading links; the page does not add empirical findings or new claims about these models.

## Fixed anchors and expanded method pools

The current methods presentation retains two fixed anchors per chapter: RCT and
quasi-experimental designs; systematic review and meta-analysis; effect size and
the Campbellian validity framework. Four additional links are sampled without
replacement from the corresponding 13-, 10- and 12-entry pools. Fixed entries
are excluded from those pools. All 41 selectable entries already exist in the vault.

The traversal began with the RCT, Research Synthesis and Campbellian framework
entry bodies, then followed relevant methods, instruments and validity concepts.
Definitions and scope passages were read for the added candidates:

- Production: random assignment, true experimental design, efficacy/effectiveness
  trials, multi-arm trials, mixed-method experimental design, intent-to-treat
  analysis, propensity-score matching and difference-in-differences.
- Synthesis: narrative synthesis, framework synthesis, umbrella review,
  comparative meta-synthesis, three-level meta-analysis and random-effects models.
- Appraisal: internal/external/construct validity, publication bias, internal and
  external validity threats, Study DIAD and the gating procedure.

Supporting Argument passages were checked in Edovald and Nevill (2021), Higgins
(2016), Cohen et al. (2011), chapter 10, Hagevold et al. (2026) and Wolf et al.
(2020). These are relevance checks for existing reading links, not new empirical
claims or a rereading of the original PDFs.

## Project discovery and compact reading revision

The project traversal started with EEF, Evidence for ESSA and Best Evidence
Encyclopedia, then followed Success for All and Reading Recovery into their
intervention examples and supporting Arguments. Slavin (2019) supplied additional
mathematics examples, including ROOTS, Number Rockets and Fraction Face-Off!.
Twenty-five existing Fact entries are included in a distinct project discovery pool:
12 intervention or curriculum programmes, four trial cases, six research/cooperation
projects and three funding programmes. Entry definitions and institutional-context
passages were read before inclusion. Reading routes include Slavin (2019), Edovald
and Nevill (2021), Gorard et al. (2020), Wadhwa et al. (2024), and Rickinson et al.
(2022b). The historical terminated incentive/pay trial is labelled as such. Inclusion
provides a reading case and makes no claim that every intervention was effective.
Unrelated semiconductor-fund links were not followed; the absent ABRACADABRA entry
and the CIRC pedagogical concept were not manufactured as Fact programme pages.

Each methods chapter now displays two fixed entries plus one random candidate.
The synthesis anchors are Meta-analysis and Meta-meta-analysis; Systematic Review
moves into the ten-item synthesis pool. School Enquiry Cycle and PRISMA were added
as the second main entries in problem formation and search/screening respectively.
Their definition passages and Brown et al. (2017) / the PRISMA entry's cited methods
context support these reading positions. All six use stages now contain two entries.
